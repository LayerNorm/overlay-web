## Title block

- Label: `Manifesto`
- H1: `AI should amplify human potential, not replace it.`
- Sub: `Overlay exists because the best models, tools, files, and workflows should meet in one open interface that people and institutions can actually control — and because the durable unit of that work is the agent, not the chat.`

## Principles (numbered prose)

1. **Start with the outcome.** Ask what work the agent should own before exposing models, tools, or orchestration.
2. **Treat agents as first-class workers.** Every agent has a persistent identity, responsibilities, resources, access, status, and history.
3. **Separate identity from execution.** An agent can change runtimes — Overlay Cloud, your machine, another harness — without losing its threads, knowledge, permissions, routines, or role.
4. **Meet people where they work.** Teams invoke agents and receive results through the surfaces they already use. Overlay remains the place to configure and supervise.
5. **Make autonomy legible and governable.** Show what an agent is doing, what it changed, what it costs, when it needs approval, and how to stop or correct it.
6. **Prove economic value.** Optimize for reliable completed outcomes and measurable return — not message volume or time spent in the interface.

## North star (closing prose)

`Overlay should increase the number and quality of useful outcomes an organization can produce with the people, knowledge, and capital it already has. Reduce the time from describing a job to receiving the first verified result — then make that result repeatable with less human intervention.`
