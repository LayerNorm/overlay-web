The control plane

Autonomy you can govern.

Chat is a surface. Overlay owns everything around the work — creation, deployment, context, access, scheduling, approvals, observability, cost, audit, and improvement.

Deploy anywhere

Overlay Cloud, your machine, or another harness. Same agent, same identity.

Context & access

Knowledge, credentials, tools, and permissions — granted per agent, revocable anytime.

Schedules & routines

Show it once, it runs on its own next time. No re-prompting, no rebuilt context.

Approvals & audit

What it did, what it changed, what it costs — and how to stop or correct it.

Observability & cost

Runs, exceptions, approval burden, time saved. Optimized for completed outcomes, not messages.

Real capabilities

Browsers, terminals, software, APIs, and connected computers for actual knowledge work.
