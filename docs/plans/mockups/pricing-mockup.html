Questions

Is free or paid a setting on my account?

No. Each workspace is free or paid on its own. You can have a free workspace and a paid one under the same account.

What counts as a paid resource?

Premium models, Overlay Cloud computers, web search, browser tasks, image and video generation, and storage beyond the free 10 MB.

How many free workspaces can I be part of?

Up to three at a time. That keeps free workspaces fair for everyone. Add a plan to any workspace whenever you want more.

How is usage billed?

Each resource is metered on its own and added up as you go. The usage view lists the quantity and the cost for every one.

Can I change my deposit or limit later?

Yes. Add credit, switch between hard stop and automatic top-ups, or change the limit at any time in your workspace’s billing settings.
