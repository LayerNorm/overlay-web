title: "Settings"
description: "A tour of the settings pages and what each one controls."
---

Open **Settings** from the menu at the bottom of the sidebar. Search at the top finds a page or a single setting by name. **Back to app** returns to where you were.

| Page | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Theme (light, dark or system) and everyday preferences. |
| **Account** | Your name, email, sign-in methods and deleting your account. |
| **Workspace** | The workspace name, People, Teams, Guests, Roles & permissions, Sharing & links, Billing and Import. Most of this is for owners and admins. |
| **Customization** | How the app looks, including the sidebar layout. |
| **Shortcuts** | Every keyboard shortcut. `⌘K` (`Ctrl+K`) opens search from anywhere. |
| **Memories** | What Overlay remembers. See [Memories](/help/memory). |
| **Providers** | Your own model provider keys, if your workspace allows them. |
| **Webhooks** | Send Overlay events to your own systems. |
| **Environments** and **Agents** | The computers and accounts behind Claude Code and Codex agents. See [Claude Code and Codex agents](/help/other-agents). |
| **Connected apps** | ChatGPT, Claude and other apps using your workspace, and personal tokens. See [Connect other apps](/help/connect-other-apps). |
| **Computers** | Cloud computers you and your agents use: open, stop or start them. |
| **Archived** | Everything you archived. Restore it, or delete it for good. |
| **Contact** | Reach the Overlay team. |

Some pages appear only when your workspace has the feature, or when your role can change it.

## Workspace roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing and deleting the workspace. |
| **Admin** | Manage people, settings and billing. |
| **Member** | Use the workspace and share into it, within the workspace's sharing settings. |
| **Guest** | See only the chats and items shared with them. |

Owners and admins decide what members may do, such as inviting people, creating agents or sharing into the workspace, in **Workspace → Sharing & links**.
