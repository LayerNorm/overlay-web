---

## Account

- Update your name, email, and profile picture.
- Sign out from all devices if needed.

## Billing

- View your subscription and usage.
- Update payment methods and auto top-up settings.
- Download invoices.

## Integrations

- Connect apps such as Gmail, Calendar, Notion, GitHub, Slack, and more.
- Each integration is scoped to your account.

## Preferences

- **Theme** — light, dark, or system.
- **Default model** — choose the model for Ask and Act modes.
- **Send with Enter** — toggle enter-to-send behavior.
- **Attach files to knowledge** — choose whether uploaded files are added to knowledge by default.

## SSO

If your workspace uses SSO, sign in through your organization. Contact your admin for access issues.
