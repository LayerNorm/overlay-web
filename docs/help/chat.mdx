The **Chats** page holds every conversation you are part of. Under **Personal** are your own AI chats. Under **Workspace** are:

- **Direct messages** with a teammate or a small group.
- **Channels**, open to everyone in the workspace or to the people you add.
- **Activity**, the latest replies and mentions across your conversations.

Chats with a single agent live under that agent on the **Agents** page instead. See [Agents](/help/agents).

## Start a chat

1. Choose **New** on the Chats page.
2. Type your message. Press `Enter` to send and `Shift+Enter` for a new line.
3. Pick a model from the model picker below the message box, or keep the one you used last.

To bring an agent into a direct message or channel, mention it with `@` and its name. It answers when mentioned.

## Models

The model picker groups models by provider, with the strongest first. Star a model to keep it under **Favorites**. Small icons show which models can read images and which reason before answering.

Free workspaces use **Auto**, which picks a free model for you, within a weekly limit. A paid workspace can use every model, charged to its credit. See [Plans and credit](/help/billing).

## Attachments

Drag files onto the message box or use the attach button. Images go to models that can read them; documents are read as text. Attached files are saved in **Files**, so you can refer to them later.

## While the AI works

- Tool calls fold into one **Worked for…** row above the answer. Click it to see each step.
- When a tool needs your permission, an approval card appears. The chat continues once you allow or deny it.
- Choose **Stop** to end a reply early. Hover a message for copy, retry, edit and delete.

## Find an old chat

Search from the top of the Chats list, or press `⌘K` (`Ctrl+K` on Windows) to search chats, files and notes together. Archived chats are under **Settings → Archived**.
