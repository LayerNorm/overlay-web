Overlay transactional emails

Every template rendered by renderLifecycleEmail() in src/shared/email/lifecycle-email.ts, with representative event data. All variants share one HTML shell — Libre Baskerville, 520px column, single “Open Overlay” CTA.

Sign-up verification and password-reset emails are hosted by WorkOS AuthKit and are not part of this set. automation.succeeded has no email destination and produces no template.
